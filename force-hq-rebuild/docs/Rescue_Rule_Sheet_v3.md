# Force HQ rescue: rule sheet v3 (12 Nov 2026, for Tom to mark up)

Goal: low entrance, high ceiling. Fewer rules, all of them visible. The rescue can be finished in three legs. The Gumdrop stays 6,300 N and Smooshy's limit stays 60 N, so the maths in Parts 1 to 4 does not change. The tools get stronger instead.

## The map
Left to right: crater floor with the Gumdrop, a gentle lower slope, a vertical cliff about 6 m high near the middle, a gentle upper slope, then the dotted line where the Big Jelly's beam reaches. The cliff splits the route: something has to lift the Gumdrop up it (crane or screw jack), then something pulls it on from the top.

## The one rule of motion
The Gumdrop moves when the pull Smooshy's machines give is at least the force the move needs. Every rope shows both, live: "Needs 1,260 N. Your machines give 60 N x 24 = 1,440 N. It moves."
- Straight lift: needs 6,300 N.
- On a ramp, or on a cart on sloping ground: needs 6,300 x rise / length.
- Dragged over bare ground: the slope force plus 2,100 N of friction, and it scrapes and can tip the hull.
- Smooshy gives 60 N x total MA.

## Connecting
Every machine has a load hook and a pull hook. Any pull hook joins any load hook, in any order. A hook with nothing on it pulses and its rope hangs slack. One pull from start to stop is a leg. A ghost Gumdrop shows where each leg ends, and the next leg starts there.

## Tools, one clear job each
| Tool | Job | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Ramp | A slope to pull up | MA = length / rise |
| Cart (wheel and axle under a platform) | Rolls over ground with almost no friction, so the ground is the ramp | needs only the slope force |
| Crane gantry | Lifts straight up, carries across, lowers | MA 1, needs pulleys and a winch |
| Screw jack | Under a platform, raises the platform and what is on it | MA 120 on its own, but a long way to turn |
| Lever | Tips the Gumdrop up onto a cart or a low platform | MA = effort arm / load arm |
| Pulley block | Multiplies the pull | 2 to 8 ropes |
| Winch | Multiplies the pull; a longer handle is a bigger MA | x4 to x20 |
| Platform | A shelf to rest on or stand machines on | every size holds the Gumdrop; size is width only |
| Dynamite | Lowers the crest or cuts a notch in the cliff | preview of the hole and the hull-risk ring before you commit |

A pulley block with 8 ropes and a x16 winch gives MA 128, enough for a straight lift at 49 N. So a leg is one machine plus one or two helpers.

## Three-leg examples
- Cart up the lower slope, crane up the cliff, ramp to the line.
- Ramp to the cliff foot, screw jack platform up the cliff, cart up the upper slope.
- Lever onto a cart, cart to the cliff, crane up and across, cart on to the line (four legs, cheaper kit).

## Training rock
Before the rescue: move a small rock with a lever, then pick it up with a crane from where the lever left it. Then the rock is gone. No boulder rules in the rescue.

**Packed away (Tom, 12 Nov 2026: "make sure after the training, that the machines used to move the boulder are packed away").** When the rock is clear, the training machines fold up and leave the board by themselves, with a short "Training kit packed away" moment. The training kit is free: it never counts against the budget, and nothing from training is left on the board, in the route strip or in the score. The rescue starts on a clean crater with the full budget.

## Budget
The brief says why: GASA can only fly so much kit to Glimmer-9, so the job is the cheapest, lightest design that keeps Smooshy's pull low. A cost meter sits beside the tray.

## Picking pieces
Hit areas follow the drawn shape. A row of chips, one per piece, picks anything without hunting through layers.

## Removed
Boulder rules and the torn leg, hidden machine roles, platform strength limits, the long tutorial. Hull damage stays simple: dragging scrapes and can tip it, falls dent it.

## Added 12 Nov 2026: the pole (Tom)
A pole replaces the pull post. Pulley blocks and winches never float: a pulley block hangs from the top arm of a pole (or from a machine's own frame), and a winch is bolted low on it where Smooshy can reach the handle. A pole with a rope hooked to the Gumdrop is a pull post (MA 1).
