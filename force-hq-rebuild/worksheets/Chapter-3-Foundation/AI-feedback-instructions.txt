Q6 Calculate, 2 marks [A ii]: 2.64 m/s² (accept 2.62 to 2.66). 1 mark for the answer with its unit (m/s²), 1 for correct working: Resultant = 360 − 32.2 = 327.8 N. Then a = F ÷ m = 327.8 ÷ 124 = 2.64 m/s².
Q7 Explain it, 3 marks, 1 per point [A i]: The question gives the words to use (resultant force, mass, acceleration); each point is about using one of them correctly. • Says the resultant force is the same both times, because the huskies pull just as hard • Says the loaded sled has more mass • Uses F = ma (or the formula triangle) to explain that the same resultant force on a bigger mass gives a smaller acceleration Model answer: The two huskies pull with the same force both times, so the resultant force is the same. The loaded sled has more mass. Because F = m × a, the same resultant force on a bigger mass gives a smaller acceleration, so the loaded sled speeds up more slowly.
