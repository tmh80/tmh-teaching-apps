Q5 Fill the gaps, 3 marks, 1 per gap [A i]: gap 1: still (also accept at rest, stationary, not moving); gap 2: velocity (also accept speed); gap 3: force (also accept any correct scientific word).
Q6 Explain it, 3 marks, 1 per point [A ii]: The question gives the words to use (inertia, keeps moving, unbalanced force); each point is about using one of them correctly. • Says the passenger was moving forwards at the same velocity as the bus • Uses inertia to explain that the passenger keeps moving forwards when the bus slows down • States that an unbalanced force (from a pole, a seat or their feet) has to act on the passenger to bring them to a stop Model answer: The passenger was moving forwards at the same velocity as the bus. When the bus brakes, the bus slows down, but nothing slows the passenger straight away. Because of their inertia, the passenger keeps moving forwards. To stop them, an unbalanced force has to act on them, such as the pull of a pole they grab or friction on their feet.
