Rescue core board

A bare test of the new rules. Get the Gumdrop from the crater floor up to the dotted line. The Gumdrop weighs 6,300 N and Smooshy can pull 60 N.

How this board works

One rule. The Gumdrop moves when the pull from Smooshy's machines is at least the force the move needs. Each leg shows both numbers.

Hooks. A machine's rope must be hooked to the Gumdrop. A slack, pulsing rope is not hooked. Drag the hook onto the Gumdrop, or use the button on the machine's card. Pulley blocks hang from a pole or a machine's frame, and winches are bolted to one. They clip onto a pull hook, or onto each other, in any order.

Legs. One pull from start to stop is a leg. A ghost Gumdrop shows where each leg ends. The next machine picks it up from there.

Poles. A pole stands on the ground. With a rope hooked to the Gumdrop it is a pull post. Set its height in its card.

Picking pieces. Tap a piece on the board, or tap its name in the row under the board.

Simplified for this test: the cart fits under the Gumdrop by itself and stays with it. Platforms, the lever, dynamite and the training rock are not on this board yet.
