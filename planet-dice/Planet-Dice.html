Planet Dice Missions
👩‍🏫 For teachers: how to use Planet Dice in class, and the ACARA standards it coversShow

Planet Dice is an interactive simulator for the Grade 7/8 Earth in Space unit (Cycle B). Students discover planets with dice rolls, work out their numbers, check each other’s working and explain what the planet would be like. It can run as a structured lesson or as an extension task.

🧭 How to use it in class

👥 Pair mode (recommended in class)

Students work in pairs. The researcher discovers a planet and works out its numbers. They then swap laptops and become each other’s analyst: they predict ✓ or ✗ for each number before checking it, then talk through every mistake together. This builds accountability and discussion.

🧑‍🚀 Solo mode

Students do both jobs, so it suits homework, absent students and early finishers. They submit their Mission Log working and it is checked automatically. Mistakes are fixed and resubmitted.

⏱️ As a 45-minute review lesson

  1. Demo Earth together (☀️ Our Solar System, travel to Earth): 5 min
  2. Researchers discover a planet and work out its numbers: 15 min
  3. Swap laptops: analysts check, then do the 🧪 Lab report and 2 So what? questions: 15 min
  4. Group briefings with the briefing script: 8 min
  5. Exit: which So what? question was hardest?: 2 min

The printed worksheet and OneNote page still work alongside the app.

🚀 As an extension

  • 🌌 Formation story: a 10-minute predict-then-watch starter on the nebula theory
  • 🛠️ Planet Forge (150 XP): students design planets, and choices that break physics are 🔒 locked with a reason
  • 🧪 Station Lab challenges, e.g. find a methane ocean
  • 🔬 Advanced tide watch (200 XP): moons on separate orbits

✅ Collecting and checking work

  • Students press ⬇ Save in the 📓 Mission Log to download their log, then hand it in on Toddle.
  • ⭐ Stars left show how independently a planet was completed (hints cost stars). 🚀 XP and rank show overall effort.
  • Real planets (☀️ Our Solar System) are ideal for modelling and practice. They earn no XP, and their names are locked.
  • Everything is saved in the browser on that laptop. A different laptop or a cleared browser starts fresh, so have students save their Mission Log at the end of each lesson.
  • The offline file works without internet or Google Fonts, so it runs reliably on school laptops.

The standards are listed here for teacher planning only. Students do not need them to play.

Planet Dice

Researcher: discover your planet one feature at a time and copy each roll and result onto your Mission Log (on paper, or the 📓 tab on the right). Every card has a 📖 table button and a 🔎 “where to look” note. Analyst: use the Analyst check at the bottom. (d8 = an 8-sided die, d6 = a 6-sided die.)

🌊 Tide watch

A side view of the coast (left) and the planet from above (right). Each “day” has two high tides. Over a “month”, watch the tides grow and shrink as the moons move round. The sea bulges towards the moons (and on the far side too). In this game all the moons pull together, so they are drawn in a line.

🧮 Work it out (researcher)

Do each step on your Mission Log (on paper, or the 📓 tab on the right). The 🔎 note tells you exactly where each number comes from. Press a 📖 button to open that table.

    🔍 Analyst check

    Step 1: Check the working. For each number: type the researcher’s number (it fills in by itself if they used the 📓 Mission Log), decide ✓ or ✗ yourself, then press Check. Correct calls earn XP, and every error you catch earns a 🎯 bounty.
